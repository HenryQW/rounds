# Band and pane
The plugin draws the band above your prompt. The band is always one line: the Bartenders that fit, each one pill `<dot> <KEY or repo>` (or `🟢 N idle`) with the status chip and the PR (the status code plain text inside it), then `+N`; a click on a pill's `<dot> <KEY or repo>` opens that Bartender in split view, and pills that do not all fit drop their PR links first; at the right end `N waits on you` and `N to act` in red (each only above zero) and Open Pane. Open Pane, `+N` and `N to act` open the `bouncer` pane beside the chat or bring it to the front. The pane has a header row (**Bouncer**, the repo; on the right `Queue N`; no dot strip, as each card shows its dot), the red `Undecided N` list (every line of `questions.md`, oldest first: `🔴 <TICKET>: <question>` and its age; a click on a Bartender's line opens its session in split view, a click on a `self` line scrolls your transcript to your last reply that holds `Decide` and the line's key; only while a line waits), the `N to act` list (the ledger items that `bouncer-ledger due` prints, run once a turn; each its key, linked to the PR or the Linear issue, its next step and Ask Bouncer, which asks the Bouncer about it), then `Bartenders N` and one card an active Bartender, all idle ones in one card, two a row, of equal width and of equal height in each row; a click on a card's dot, its session title or its non-key name opens that Bartender in split view. A card is a Rounds session row. Row 1: `<dot> <session title>` (the ticket title, or the repo of an idle or old-format title; `🟢 N idle` for the idle group), one button that opens the session, and `⋯`. Row 2: the key (a link to its Linear issue with the `linear_workspace_url` option), the status code as a chip and, at the right end, the time since the session's last activity (`28m`). Row 3: every open PR of the ticket (its open `pr` ledger items, and the session's own PR while it is open or while the ticket's report or ledger names it; a merged or closed PR of an earlier task drops off), each a link in its state colour with its base while open (`⑂ #17860 → dev`; `gh pr view` once a turn); the band's pill shows `⑂ #17860 +1` instead. Row 4: the next step of the ticket's first open ledger item (a `pr` item whose PR is merged or closed is skipped), else the QUESTION of its last `bouncer-report` while it waits on the user (NEEDS_INPUT, BLOCKED) or its RESULT, else nothing. The PR links to the full GitHub URL that the Bartender's last report or its ticket's ledger lines name (else it is plain text, as a folder may be another repo than the PR), so put the full PR URL in EVIDENCE; its glyph coloured by PR state (green open, purple merged, grey closed). Off the terminal the band and the pane take Linear's design tokens: each Bartender is a card (surface, hairline border, round corners), all cards of equal width, for the system's dark or light appearance; Open Pane is quiet dim text. The terminal draws the cards with round borders. Text that carries information is never dim. The band reads your linked sessions (`list_sessions`) at session start and after each of your turns, for the titles, last activity, PRs and whether a turn runs. A click on a card's `⋯` shows Status, Stop and Reset on one row under row 2, as three buttons of equal width; Reset is red, and Stop and Reset send only after a second click on `Confirm …?` (Cancel or 5 seconds goes back). So keep RESULT to one short line. A dot comes from the title, the last `bouncer-report` STATUS of that Bartender's ticket and whether its session runs a turn, first match wins: see the dot table in "Band" of SKILL.md.
