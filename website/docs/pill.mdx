The pill is a small tab at a screen edge. It shows the state of your sessions at a glance.

## States

| Pill | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Clear glass | No session runs and none needs you. |
| Blue, with a count | Sessions are running. The count is the number running. |
| Orange, with a count | A session needs your input. If you have not seen it, the pill peeks out of the edge and shakes a cocktail shaker. |
| Red, with a count | A session failed. If you have not seen it, the pill peeks out of the edge, and its glass shakes and breaks. Red shows before orange. |
| Green, with a count | Only results wait: Ready, Finished or Stopped sessions you have not opened. The pill is still. |

The count is always the Last Call count: when the pill shows 3, Last Call shows 3. A session you opened is Seen: a result leaves Last Call, and a wait stays until the agent goes on, but the pill no longer peeks for it. When the pill has a colour, the session window opens on Last Call.

## Use the pill

- Hover or click the pill to open the [session window](/session-window).
- Right-click the pill for Settings and Quit Rounds.
- Hold ⌘ and drag the pill to move it along the edge. A hover with ⌘ down does not open the window.

## Displays

In Settings, the Pill page sets the screen edge (Left or Right) and the displays that show a pill. If you check no connected display, the built-in display keeps the pill. The window opens on the display whose pill you used.
