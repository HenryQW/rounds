Open Settings from the gear button in the session window, with ⌘, or from the pill's right-click menu.

## General

Open at login, the app that opens a session, and the session window shortcut. Click the shortcut field and press a combination with ⌘, ⌥ or ⌃. Press Delete to clear it.

## Filters

What Last Call counts and when a session is Aged. Last Call always shows a session that needs input or failed until the agent goes on, and a result (Ready, Finished, Stopped) until you open it. Count sub-agents in Last Call is off, because the agent that started a sub-agent asks you itself. Move to Aged after sets how long a quiet session stays out of Aged: 1 day by default, in hours or days.

## Report a Bug

At the foot of the sidebar, Report a Bug opens a prefilled GitHub issue page in your browser. It lists the Rounds version, macOS, and the versions of Claude, Codex and herdr. You read it and send it yourself.
